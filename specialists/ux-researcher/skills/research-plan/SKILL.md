# Research plan

## Steps

1. Clarify **decisions** with Chief (not vague “learn about users”).
2. Choose method: interviews, usability test, survey, diary (as appropriate).
3. Define recruitment, n, timeline, and ethics/consent.
4. Get Chief sign-off on plan before sessions.

Use `templates/research-plan.md`.
