# Content strategy

## When to use

- Large onboarding or education surface
- Help/docs inside product
- Chief scopes “content system” not just strings

## Deliverables

- Content map: user journey × content types (tooltip, modal, email, etc.)
- Prioritized backlog of content work
- Governance: who updates copy when product changes

## Handoff

Link content map in handoff packet; Chief aligns with Research priorities.
